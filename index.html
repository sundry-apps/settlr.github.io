Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Settlr free to use?

Yes, Settlr is completely free to download on iOS and Android. You can create groups, log shared expenses, and simplify balances without hidden paywalls or subscription barriers.

Does Settlr transfer real money or link bank accounts?

No. Settlr is an expense tracking and balance calculator tool. It calculates who owes whom and simplifies debt amounts. Actual payments (via cash, Revolut, Venmo, bank transfer, etc.) take place externally and are recorded in Settlr with one tap.

Can I split bills by custom percentages or unequal amounts?

Absolutely. Settlr allows you to split each expense equally, by exact dollar amounts, or by custom percentages, ensuring everyone pays only their fair share.

How do I invite my friends or roommates to a group?

You can share an instant invite link or group code via WhatsApp, iMessage, Messenger, or email. Members can join as guests or registered accounts.
